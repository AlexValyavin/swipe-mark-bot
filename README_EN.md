## The problem

We save everything:

- YouTube videos
- Instagram posts
- TikToks
- articles
- Telegram messages
- things we want to read "later"

And eventually we have hundreds of saves that we never revisit.

## The idea

SwipeMark turns your saved content into a simple swipeable inbox.

Open a card.
Decide what to do with it.
Move on.

## Features

- 📥 Save from Telegram
- 👆 Swipe-based sorting
- 🔗 Links / YouTube / Instagram / TikTok
- 📝 Telegram messages
- 📁 Folders & tags
- ✨ AI summaries
- 🤖 AI-assisted organization
- 📱 Telegram Mini App
- 🌐 PWA

## How it works

1. Send something to the bot
2. Open SwipeMark
3. Swipe through your saves
4. Keep, archive or postpone
5. Find everything later
